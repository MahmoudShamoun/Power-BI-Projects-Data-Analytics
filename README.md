### 2️⃣ E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard
**Description:**
An end-to-end e-commerce analytics project built on the Northwind dataset. The report spans 3 interactive pages - Sales Dashboard, Customer Dashboard, and an Insights page - covering revenue performance, geographic distribution, product rankings, and customer behavior analysis across 21 countries and 69 cities.

**Key Features:**
* Revenue tracking: Net Sales ($1.27M), Gross Revenue ($1.35M), and Discount impact ($89K)
* Sales trends by month, year, city, and country with drill-down capability
* Top 5 products and 8 category breakdown by net sales
* Customer analysis: top 10 companies, order distribution by country and contact role
* 3-page navigable report with consistent slicers (Year, Cities, Month) and cross-filtering
* Dedicated Insights page summarising 8 key business findings

**Skills Used:**
Power BI, DAX, Data Modeling (Snowflake Schema), Power Query, Data Visualization

**Purpose:**
Demonstrates the ability to model transactional e-commerce data, build multi-page reports, and extract actionable business insights from sales and customer data.

**🔗 Live Dashboard:** [E-Commerce Project Dashboard](https://app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiZmMxMTcwOTctNTc4Zi00Y2FiLTkyY2ItZDk0OThkODYwY2MzIiwidCI6ImY0ZTg5MTY4LTU4NGUtNGE3MS04MDAxLWE1OWRlMmQ1MThjYiJ9)
